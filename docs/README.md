# Documents

- `GALIL-Brand-Refresh-Options.pptx` — brand refresh options for the website.
- `deprecated/` — specifications, rollout guides and demo films of WorkDesk
  and Taskana from before the Employee Portal. Out of date; see
  [`deprecated/README.md`](deprecated/README.md).

- `films/Do-You-Suffer-from-AppSheet.mp4` — a three-minute medicine-commercial
  parody for the CEO and Operations on why AppSheet goes and the Employee Portal stays.
  Hebrew and English subtitles are inside the file (choose them in the
  player) and beside it as `.srt` files.

- `films/GALIL-Employee-Portal-Commercial.mp4` — a one-minute commercial for the Employee Portal
  and all its apps, cut to a generated rock anthem. Made with `tools/commercial/`.

- `films/GALIL-Employee-Portal-A-Day-at-GALIL.mp4` — the calmer, more formal commercial: one working
  day from 08:00 to 18:00, an app for each hour, with piano and strings. Made with `tools/commercial/`.

- Tutorial films of the Employee Portal — every screen, on a computer and on a phone — live
  beside the Handbook in `Employee Portal/handbook/films/` and play inside its chapters and
  the matching Knowledge Base articles. They are made with `tools/tutorials/`.

Other new documents and films for the Employee Portal are still to be made. Until
then, the current reference is the Handbook, the Knowledge Base and the
administrator section of the install guide, all inside `Employee Portal/`.
