Install WorkDesk and Taskana on your phone

Both apps install straight from the browser, with no app store. Each app gets its own icon on your home screen, opens full screen and signs in with your GALIL Microsoft account. It takes about a minute per app.

1. Open the app you want to install

WorkDesk

Hours, plans, tasks, expenses and budgets.

Open WorkDesk
On a computer? Scan this with your phone's camera to open WorkDesk there.

Taskana

Projects and tasks for the writing teams.

Open Taskana
On a computer? Scan this with your phone's camera to open Taskana there.

2. Add it to your home screen

  1. Open the app's link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (top right on iPad). On iOS 26, tap ••• first, then Share.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen. If you don't see it, tap Edit Actions at the bottom of the list and add it.
  4. Leave Open as Web App switched on, keep the name (WorkDesk or Taskana) and tap Add.
  5. Open the app from the new icon on your home screen and sign in with your GALIL Microsoft account.

Sign in from the icon. On iPhone the home-screen app keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari, so sign in once more after you open it from the icon.

Using Chrome or Edge on iPhone?

On iOS 16.4 and later, tap Share (in the address bar or the menu) and then Add to Home Screen. If it isn't offered, use Safari.

  1. Open the app's link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮ in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then Install. (Some phones show Install app directly in the menu, or a banner at the bottom of the page. Either one does the same thing.)
  4. Confirm with Install (or Add). The icon appears on your home screen and in your app drawer.
  5. Open the app from the icon and sign in with your GALIL Microsoft account.

Samsung Internet

Tap the menu ≡, then Add page to and Home screen.

Microsoft Edge

Tap the menu •••, then Add to phone and Install.

  1. Open the app's link in Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome.
  2. Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar. Or open the menu ••• / ⋮: in Edge choose Apps → Install this site as an app; in Chrome choose Cast, save and share → Install page as app.
  3. Click Install. The app opens in its own window.
  4. To keep it handy, right-click its icon on the taskbar and choose Pin to taskbar (on a Mac, Options → Keep in Dock).

Safari on a Mac

Choose File → Add to Dock (macOS Sonoma or later).

Then do the same for the other app

Each app installs separately. Go back to step 1 and repeat for the second one. You'll end up with two icons:

WorkDesk
Taskana

Questions

I don't see "Add to Home Screen" or "Install".
You're probably inside another app's built-in browser (Outlook, Teams, WhatsApp…). Tap its menu and choose Open in Safari / Open in Chrome, then try again.
Do I need to update the apps?
No. They update themselves the next time you open them with an internet connection.
The icon shows a letter or a screenshot instead of the WorkDesk / Taskana icon.
Remove the shortcut, reload the page once in the browser, then add it again.
How do I remove an app?
Touch and hold the icon, then choose Remove App (iPhone) or Uninstall / Remove (Android). On a computer, open the app, click its ⋯ menu and choose Uninstall.
Something else isn't working?
Contact the GALIL WorkDesk administrator and tell them which phone and browser you're using.
